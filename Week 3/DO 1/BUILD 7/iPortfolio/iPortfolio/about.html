About GreenLeaf

Creative digital solutions for growing businesses.

Helping Businesses Grow Online

GreenLeaf Digital Studio is a fictional creative digital agency based in Kolkata, India. We help small businesses build a strong and professional online presence.

Our team focuses on creating attractive websites, developing online stores and building consistent brand identities. We believe that every business deserves a website that represents its ideas and values.

Our goal is to make digital solutions simple, useful and accessible for growing businesses.
